A 70 kg adult has total body water equal to 60% of body mass, and one-third of it is extracellular. What is their extracellular fluid volume?

<!-- split:hints -->

- First find total body water.
- Extracellular fluid is one-third of total body water.

<!-- split:explanation -->

$TBW = 0.6 \times 70 = 42$ L, so $ECF = 42 / 3 = 14$ L (and ICF ≈ 28 L).
