Estimate the total body water of an 80 kg adult, taking body water as 60% of body mass.

<!-- split:hints -->

- 1 kg of water is about 1 L.
- Total body water ≈ 0.6 × body mass.

<!-- split:explanation -->

$TBW \approx 0.6 \times 80 = 48$ L.
