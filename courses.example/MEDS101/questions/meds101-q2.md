Estimate the osmolarity of a 0.15 mol/L sodium chloride solution, assuming NaCl dissociates completely.

<!-- split:hints -->

- Each NaCl gives two particles: Na⁺ and Cl⁻.
- Multiply the molarity by the number of particles, then convert mol to mOsm.

<!-- split:explanation -->

$0.15 \text{ mol/L} \times 2 = 0.30$ Osm/L $= 300$ mOsm/L. Real NaCl doesn't dissociate quite completely, so the measured value is a little lower.
