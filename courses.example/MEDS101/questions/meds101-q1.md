For each ATP it uses, what does the Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase move across the plasma membrane?

<!-- split:hints -->

- Inside the cell, Na⁺ is low and K⁺ is high. The pump keeps it that way.
- The pump is electrogenic: it moves more positive charge out than in.

<!-- split:explanation -->

The pump moves **3 Na⁺ out and 2 K⁺ in** per ATP. Both ions move against their gradients, which is why it needs ATP, and the uneven exchange makes the inside of the cell slightly more negative.
