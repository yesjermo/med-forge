Red blood cells are placed in 0.45% sodium chloride (half the concentration of normal saline). What happens to them?

<!-- split:hints -->

- Is 0.45% NaCl isotonic, hypotonic or hypertonic compared with the inside of a red cell?
- Water moves towards the side with more dissolved particles.

<!-- split:explanation -->

0.45% NaCl is **hypotonic**: the cell interior has more solute, so water enters by osmosis. The cells swell, and some burst (haemolysis). In a hypertonic solution they would shrink and crenate instead.
