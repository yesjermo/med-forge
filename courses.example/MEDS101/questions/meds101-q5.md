Which of these is an example of **positive** feedback?

<!-- split:hints -->

- Negative feedback reverses a change; positive feedback makes it bigger.
- Which response leads to *more* of the original stimulus?

<!-- split:explanation -->

During labour, cervical stretch triggers oxytocin release, which strengthens contractions and stretches the cervix further: the response amplifies the stimulus until birth ends the loop. The other three responses all oppose the original change, so they are negative feedback.
