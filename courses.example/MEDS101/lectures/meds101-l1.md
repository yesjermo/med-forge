- The plasma membrane is a phospholipid bilayer; small non-polar molecules cross it directly
- Ions and polar molecules need channels or carriers (facilitated diffusion)
- Active transport moves substances against their gradient and needs energy (ATP directly, or an ion gradient)
- Osmolarity counts particles: multiply molarity by the particles per formula unit
- Tonicity is about what happens to the cell: isotonic (no change), hypotonic (swells), hypertonic (shrinks)
