- Homeostasis keeps variables near a set point
- Negative feedback opposes change (temperature, glucose, blood pressure); positive feedback amplifies it (labour, clotting)
- Total body water ≈ 60% of body mass: two-thirds intracellular, one-third extracellular
- Extracellular fluid is about three-quarters interstitial and one-quarter plasma
